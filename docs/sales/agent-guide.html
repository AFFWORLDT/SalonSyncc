Chapter 1

Our vision and mission

Every salon, however small, should be able to run like a well-organised business: clear numbers, fair pay for the team and customers who come back.

Vision

To be the system every salon and barber shop in the region opens first thing in the morning and closes last thing at night.

Mission

Replace the notebook, the calculator and the guesswork with one simple app that the owner, the manager and every barber can use on the phone they already have.

What we believe

Simple beats clever

A barber with wet hands should be able to ring up a bill in a few taps. If a feature needs a training session, we haven't finished building it.

Fair numbers, fewer fights

When everyone sees the same sales and commission, the end-of-month argument about who earned what disappears.

The salon's own brand

Each salon gets its own web address and logo. Their customers see the salon's name on bills and messages, not ours.

Try before you pay

Every salon gets the full product free for 7 days, no card needed. We earn the sale by being useful, not by locking anyone in.

Where SalonKit comes from

SalonKit was built with a real working barber shop in Dubai, day after day at the counter. Every feature started as a real problem: a missing bill, a commission dispute, a customer who stopped coming. That is our strongest story. When you talk to an owner, you are not selling something designed in an office. You are bringing the tools another salon already uses every day.

Chapter 2

The problem every salon has

Before you show anything, you must understand the owner's pain. These are the problems you will hear again and again. Learn them; ask about them; let the owner tell you about them in their own words.

What happens todayWhat it costs the owner
The notebook at the counterBills written by hand get lost, skipped or written wrong. Nobody can see today's total until someone adds it up at closing time.
Commission worked out by handHours with a calculator at the end of the month, and arguments with barbers who remember a different number. Good barbers leave over money they think they're owed.
Cash that doesn't matchThe drawer, the bank and the notebook never agree. The owner can't tell what is a mistake and what is missing.
The owner must be thereNo way to see the day's sales from home or another branch. The owner can't take a day off without worrying.
Customers who don't come backNo list of who visited and when. Nobody reminds a regular who hasn't been in for a month. They drift to the shop next door.
Few Google reviewsHappy customers leave without being asked. New customers searching on Google Maps choose the salon with more stars.
VAT and paperworkBills without a TRN or VAT line, and an accountant asking for numbers nobody kept.

Listen for these words

"At the end of the month…", "my barber says…", "I don't know where the cash went", "I have to be here every day", "that customer stopped coming", "my accountant wants…". Each one is a door. When you hear it, ask one more question, then show the part of SalonKit that answers it.

Chapter 3

What SalonKit is

SalonKit is salon and barber shop software. The counter rings up bills, the app works out each barber's commission, keeps the daily sheet, and helps bring customers back.

Your pitch, three lengths

10 seconds (in the door)

"SalonKit is an app for salons: bills, barber commission and the daily sheet, all on your phone. Every salon gets 7 days free."

30 seconds (the owner is listening)

"Right now you probably write bills in a book and work out commission at the end of the month. With SalonKit, your barbers ring up each bill on the phone in a few taps. The app adds up the day for you, works out every barber's commission straight away, and you can see today's sales from anywhere. It was built with a barber shop in Dubai, and you can try everything free for 7 days."

2 minutes (you have a seat)

Use the 30-second pitch, then add the one or two parts that match what the owner told you: the ledger if cash is the problem, WhatsApp reminders if customers don't come back, Google reviews if they want more customers, VAT bills if they are VAT-registered. Finish with: "Shall we set it up for your salon now? It takes five minutes and it's free for 7 days."

Chapter 4 · Product tour (1 of 3)

A tour of the product

Know every part well enough to show it in under a minute. Plan names in brackets show where each part is included.

Bills (every plan, including Free)

  • The salon's own price list: one tap per service, plus quick amounts for anything else.
  • Pick the barber, cash or card, customer name and phone (or skip them).
  • Every bill gets its own number with the salon's prefix: no gaps, no repeats.
  • Print a receipt, share a link the customer opens on their phone, or send the PDF on WhatsApp (Pro Max).
  • Owners and managers can edit or delete a bill; on Pro Max every change is kept in the audit log with who and when.

Daily sheet (every plan)

The notebook page, filled in automatically: one row per day, one column per barber, with the day's expenses, commission and profit. The business day ends at the hour the salon closes (4 am by default), so late-night bills count for the right day.

Barber commission (every plan)

  • Each barber has their own percentage, set once.
  • Every bill credits that barber's share automatically and keeps the rate it was made with: changing a rate never rewrites old months.
  • Barbers see what they earned today on their own phone.

Dashboard

Today, 7 days, 30 days, a year or any custom dates: sales, customers, expenses, commission and profit, with the change against the previous period and each barber's share.

Monthly and yearly reports (Pro)

Sales, expenses and profit month by month, next to the previous period, so the owner knows straight away whether this month is better than the last.

Excel in one click (Pro)

Expenses

The salon's expenses (rent, supplies, salaries) and each barber's own expenses, recorded where they happen. Owners can let a barber add their own expenses, or keep that to themselves.

  • Staff can sign in with their username and password, or with a code sent to their email. On the phone app they can also continue with Google, or with Apple on iPhone.

Who we sell to

Focus your time where SalonKit helps most. Start with the salons nearest you and build a cluster: owners talk to each other.

Ideal customer

Great fit

  • Barber shops and gents' salons with 3 or more chairs
  • Ladies' and unisex salons with several stylists on commission
  • Owners who are not always in the shop, or who own two or more branches
  • Salons that already use WhatsApp to talk to customers
  • VAT-registered salons that need proper tax invoices

Still worth a visit

  • One- or two-chair shops: the Free plan is a real answer
  • New salons about to open: start them right from day one
  • Salons using Excel: show what the barbers' phones add

Who decides

The owner decides and pays. The manager or head barber often influences: win them and they will sell it to the owner for you. Barbers are users; when they like ringing up their own bills and seeing their commission, the owner hears about it.

Best times to visit

TimeWhy
Weekday mornings, just after openingQuietest time; the owner is often there.
Early afternoonBetween the lunch and evening rush.
AvoidEvenings, weekends and the days before Eid and holidays: the salon is full and nobody can listen.

Chapter 10

Walking in: the first 60 seconds

Owners decide in the first minute whether to listen. Be calm, polite and quick. Respect their customers: never interrupt a haircut.

Before you open the door

  • Look in: if every chair is busy and people are waiting, come back later.
  • Clean, simple clothes. Phone charged, demo salon open, brightness up.
  • Know one thing about this salon (number of chairs, their Google rating).

The opening

"Assalamu alaikum / Good morning. I'm [name] from SalonKit. Is the owner here? … I'll only take five minutes. We make an app that does the bills and the barbers' commission for salons, and I'd like to show you how it works."

If they say "I'm busy"

"No problem at all. When is a quiet time, tomorrow morning? I'll come back then." Write the time down and come back exactly then.

If they say "We don't need anything"

"I understand. Can I ask one question: how do you work out your barbers' commission at the end of the month?" (Let them talk. Most owners have a story.)

If they say "Just leave a flyer"

"Of course. It's quicker to see it: can I show you one bill on my phone? Thirty seconds."

Your body language

  • Stand to the side of the counter, not in front of the customers.
  • Show your phone so the owner can see the screen; let them hold it.
  • Smile, slow down, and stop talking when they start.

Chapter 13

Starting the free trial on the spot

The single most important step. A salon set up during your visit is many times more likely to become a customer than one that says "I'll sign up later".

Step by step, with the owner

  1. On the owner's phone, open salonkit.online (or the SalonKit app) and tap Start free trial.
  2. Enter the salon's name, the owner's name, the owner's email, mobile and country. On the phone app the owner can also tap Continue with Google (or Apple on iPhone) instead of making a password.
  3. Choose the salon ID together: short, easy to type, for example royalcuts. It becomes their address: royalcuts.salonkit.online.
  4. Add the salon's logo (a photo of their sign works).
  5. Check the price list: a starter list is already there. Change prices and add their own services. Do it together, quickly; perfect it later.
  6. Add the barbers: name and, if they have one, their email. With an email, each barber gets an invite link that opens SalonKit already signed in.
  7. Set each barber's commission percentage.
  8. If VAT-registered: Settings → VAT on, add the TRN.
  9. Ring up one real bill together for the next customer.
  10. Show the owner the dashboard on their own phone, then leave.

Never

  • Use your own email or phone for a salon's account. The account belongs to the owner.
  • Ask for or write down the owner's password.
  • Create a salon without the owner's agreement.

One email belongs to one person in one salon. If an owner says "that email is already used", they or a staff member already have a SalonKit account: help them sign in instead (email code or "Forgot password?").

Chapter 15 · Objections (1 of 2)

Handling objections

An objection is a question in disguise. Stay calm, agree with the feeling, answer with a fact, then ask a question.

"It's too expensive."

"I understand, every dirham counts. That's why there's a Free plan, and the trial is free. Pro is 200 a month, under seven dirhams a day. How much time do you spend on commission each month? What is that evening worth to you?"

"My notebook works fine."

"It has worked for years, I agree. Can I ask: how long does month-end take, and do the barbers ever disagree with the numbers? SalonKit is the same notebook, but it adds itself up and nobody can argue with it. Try it for 7 days, keep the notebook too if you like."

"My barbers won't use it."

"Barbers usually like it most, because they see their own commission every day. Ringing up a bill is two taps. Let's add one barber now and try it on the next customer."

"I'm not good with phones / technology."

"If you can send a WhatsApp, you can use SalonKit. I'll set it up with you now, and I'll come back on day four to check everything."

"I don't have time right now."

"Of course. When is a quiet time? The setup takes five minutes; I'll come back exactly then."

"Is my data safe? Who can see my numbers?"

"Only you and the people you add. Each salon has its own address and logins; no other salon can ever see your data, and search engines don't index it."

Chapter 17

After the sale: onboarding

A customer who uses SalonKit well stays for years and sends you others. Make the first month easy.

First-month checklist

  • Every barber signs in on their own phone (invites sent to their emails).
  • Price list complete, with real prices.
  • Commission percentage set for every barber.
  • Logo added; VAT and TRN set if they are registered.
  • Google review link added in Settings (from their Google Business Profile → "Ask for reviews").
  • Owner knows the dashboard and the daily sheet.
  • Pro: owner has downloaded one Excel report.
  • Pro Max: WhatsApp Business number connected; ledger shows cash and bank.
  • Staff emails added so barbers get their own daily report.

Stay in touch

  • Visit once in the first month, at a quiet time.
  • Pass support questions to support@salonkit.online with the salon ID and a screenshot.
  • Tell us about happy salons: with their permission, their story helps sell to others.

Chapter 20

Code of conduct

You represent SalonKit in every salon you walk into. Our reputation is built one visit at a time.

Always

  • Be honest about what SalonKit does today.
  • Respect the salon's customers and time.
  • Keep owners' information private.
  • Set up accounts in the owner's name, email and phone.
  • Follow Google's rules: every customer is invited to review.
  • Report requests and problems to the team.

Never

  • Promise features, dates, discounts or terms that aren't approved.
  • Ask for, keep or type an owner's password.
  • Criticise competitors or other salons.
  • Write fake reviews, or ask salons to.
  • Send WhatsApp offers to customers without the salon's approval.
  • Pressure an owner who said no.

Customer data

Salons trust us with their sales and their customers' names and numbers. Never copy, photograph or share any salon's data. If you see a problem with an account, report it to the team; don't try to fix data yourself.

Chapter 22

Staying motivated

Field sales is a numbers game played by people. Some days every door says no. The agents who succeed are the ones who come back the next morning.

Remember why it matters

Every salon you sign up is a small business that stops losing money it can't see, barbers who get paid fairly without arguing, and an owner who can finally take a day off. You are not selling an app. You are helping someone run their business better.

Habits of top agents

They plan

A route, a list and a target every morning.

They listen

The owner talks 70% of the time.

They set up on the spot

"Let's do it now" beats "I'll send you a link".

They follow up

Day 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, every time.

They learn from every no

Write down why, and try something different next time.

They build clusters

One happy salon on a street makes the next one easier.

Your growth

Strong agents grow into team leads, trainers and area managers as SalonKit expands across the region. Talk to your manager about your path:  .

When it's hard

  • Count conversations, not sales. Sales follow conversations.
  • Call a colleague; share one story from the day.
  • Re-read the objections chapter: most "no"s are "not yet".
